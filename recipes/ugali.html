Ugali

Ingredients

  • 2 cups maize meal (cornmeal)
  • 4 cups water (adjust as needed)
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt (optional)

Instructions

  1. Bring the water to a rolling boil in a heavy saucepan.
  2. Stir in the salt if using.
  3. Reduce the heat to medium-low. Gradually sprinkle in the maize meal while stirring continuously to avoid lumps.
  4. Continue stirring until the mixture thickens and pulls away from the sides of the pan. This may take 5–10 minutes.
  5. Cover and cook on low for another 2–4 minutes, stirring once, until the ugali is firm and cooked through.
  6. Turn onto a plate or wooden board, shape with a spoon or wet hands, and serve hot.

Serving Suggestions

Serve ugali with stews, sukuma wiki (collard greens), grilled fish, or meat dishes.

Notes

  • Adjust the water-to-maize ratio for softer or firmer ugali.
  • Traditional ugali is made with plain maize meal; some regions use a blend with cassava or millet.
