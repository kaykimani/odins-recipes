Pilau

Ingredients

  • 2 cups long-grain rice (washed and drained)
  • 1 lb (450 g) beef or chicken, diced (optional)
  • 3 tablespoons vegetable oil
  • 1 large onion, finely sliced
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1-inch piece ginger, minced
  • 2 tomatoes, chopped (or 1/2 cup canned tomatoes)
  • 3-4 cups water or stock (adjust for rice type)
  • 2 teaspoons pilau masala or curry powder
  • 1 teaspoon ground cumin
  • 1 teaspoon ground coriander
  • 1/2 teaspoon turmeric (optional)
  • Salt to taste
  • Fresh cilantro for garnish (optional)

Instructions

  1. Heat the oil in a large pot over medium heat. Add the sliced onion and cook until golden brown.
  2. Add the garlic and ginger; fry briefly until fragrant.
  3. If using meat, add the diced meat and brown on all sides.
  4. Add the tomatoes and cook until they break down, about 3–5 minutes.
  5. Stir in the pilau masala, cumin, coriander, turmeric, and salt. Cook for 1–2 minutes to bloom the spices.
  6. Add the washed rice and stir to coat the grains in the spiced mixture.
  7. Pour in the water or stock (use about 1.5–2 times the rice volume depending on rice type). Bring to a boil.
  8. Reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer gently until the rice is cooked and liquid is absorbed (about 15–20 minutes).
  9. Remove from heat and let rest, covered, for 5 minutes. Fluff with a fork and garnish with cilantro before serving.

Serving Suggestions

Serve pilau with kachumbari (fresh tomato and onion salad), chutney, or a side of vegetables.

Notes

  • Adjust spice levels and meat quantity to taste.
  • For extra aroma, add a few cardamom pods, cloves, and a cinnamon stick while frying the onions.
  • Use stock instead of water for richer flavor.
