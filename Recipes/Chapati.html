Chapati

Ingredients

  • 3 cups all-purpose flour (plus extra for dusting)
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1 tablespoon oil or melted butter (optional)
  • 1 to 1 1/4 cups warm water (adjust as needed)

Instructions

  1. In a large bowl, mix the flour and salt. Add the oil if using.
  2. Gradually add warm water and mix until a soft dough forms.
  3. Knead the dough for 5–8 minutes until smooth and elastic. Cover and let rest for 20–30 minutes.
  4. Divide the dough into golf-ball sized portions and roll each into a ball. Flatten slightly.
  5. On a lightly floured surface, roll each ball into a thin circle about 6–8 inches in diameter.
  6. Heat a dry skillet or griddle over medium-high heat. Cook each chapati for about 30–60 seconds on the first side until small bubbles form, then flip.
  7. Cook the second side for another 30–60 seconds, pressing gently with a cloth or spatula; flip once more if needed to puff and brown lightly.
  8. Brush with a little butter or ghee if desired and keep warm in a cloth-lined basket until serving.

Serving Suggestions

Serve chapati warm with stews, curries, or vegetables. They can also be used as wraps.

Notes

  • For softer chapatis, add a little extra oil to the dough and roll slightly thinner.
  • Practice and even rolling help chapatis puff up nicely; a hot skillet is key.
